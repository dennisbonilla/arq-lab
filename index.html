The rule the whole diagram rests on

Every arrow above exists because all containers share one network. Inside it they find each other by name: Jenkins reaches AEM at aem65-author:4502, and the Dispatcher reaches Publish at aem65-publish:4503. From the browser you come in through the host port instead: localhost:4502. Same service, two addresses, depending on who is asking.
